Pick the border corner solution

Zoom in/out and compare corners. Option 2 is usually the cleanest when top and right colors differ.

Baseline (can show triangle)

Different border colors meet at the corner → anti-aliasing may look like a triangle.

top: #000, right: #f16

Option 1: Make both borders #000

Simplest. No artifact, but right border becomes black too.

top: #000, right: #000

Option 2: Inset shadows (recommended)

Avoids border-join rendering. Keeps right border #f16 without the triangle.

top: #000 (shadow), right: #f16 (shadow)

Option 3: Border + corner patch

Keep borders but cover the corner with a small black square (2×2).

top: #000, right: #f16 + patched corner
